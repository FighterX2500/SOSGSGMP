[h1]SoS GSG MP: multiplayer for Songs of Syx[/h1]

Each player runs their own city-state on one shared world map. Meet each other and the NPC factions through diplomacy, trade and war, with real-time battles between players. Made for a small group of friends, usually 2 or 3, in long sessions that are saved and resumed.

[b]Playtest build.[/b] Most of version 1 works; sieges of player cities and chat are still to come (see the milestone below). Expect bugs, and please send logs (at the bottom).

[h2]You need[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Songs of Syx V71, build 71.44[/b], on Windows, through Steam. Other builds aren't supported.
[*][b]SOSDET[/b], the deterministic engine this mod runs on: subscribe to it too.
[*]In the launcher's mod list, enable [b]SOSDET above SOS GSG MP[/b].
[*]Every player needs the [b]same versions[/b] of both mods. With a different version the joining PC can't load the world.
[/list]

[h2]Playing[/h2]
[list]
[*]The main menu gets a [b]Multiplayer[/b] button.
[*][b]Over Steam:[/b] the players are Steam friends. The host chooses Create Lobby, then "create Steam lobby"; the others choose "join through Steam".
[*][b]By IP:[/b] the host's TCP port 47510 must be reachable (a forwarded port, or a VPN such as Radmin VPN). The host chooses "create (IP)"; the others choose "join by IP".
[*]The host picks the map. Every player picks a race, a faction and a capital location; NPC realms are generated around you. Then each of you places your landing party, and the game starts.
[*]The host sets the speed, and the slowest PC caps it for everyone. Menus and popups never pause the game.
[/list]

[h2]What you can do[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Your own city, no delay.[/b] Every PC runs only its own city; the world map runs in lockstep on every PC.
[*][b]Diplomacy between players:[/b] click another player wherever you'd click an NPC. Peace, trade, colleagues and alliance as proposals; money, goods and regions in a deal; vassals with a tribute rate, and protectors.
[*][b]Trade:[/b] sell to each other in the market across any land at peace, each with your own tariff.
[*][b]War and real-time battles:[/b] declare war; when your armies meet, each of you commands your own army on the battle map with your own technology and bonuses, while everyone else watches. Besiege another player's region and they can sally out.
[*][b]Viewing a city:[/b] ask to watch another player's city live; they allow or refuse.
[*][b]Saves:[/b] Save Campaign in the escape menu, the host's autosave, a save before every battle. The host resumes with Load Saved Lobby.
[*][b]Drop-outs and desyncs:[/b] if a player drops out, every game stops and the host goes on without them or returns to the last save. If the worlds ever split, the host chooses Resync or Save and exit.
[/list]

[h2]Known problems[/h2]
[list]
[*]Speeds of 5x and above don't reach their full rate yet.
[*]Capitals can't be taken yet: the assault on a player's city comes later.
[*]The owner's game pauses for a few seconds when their city's view opens; everyone else's for about a second.
[*]Goods or people crossing between a city and the world in the last half second before a campaign save can be lost when that save is resumed.
[*]Two players can end up with the same realm name.
[/list]

[h2]Bug reports[/h2]
Every PC writes a log of each session. After a crash, a desync window or anything odd, close the game and send these folders, with a line about what happened and roughly when:
[list]
[*][b]%APPDATA%\songsofsyx\sosgsgmp-logs[/b]: the session logs
[*][b]%APPDATA%\songsofsyx\logs[/b]: the game's crash files
[/list]
Every desync is a bug we want to see.
