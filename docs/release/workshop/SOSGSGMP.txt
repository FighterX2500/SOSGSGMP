[h1]SoS GSG MP: multiplayer for Songs of Syx[/h1]

Each player runs their own city-state on one shared world map. Meet each other and the NPC factions through diplomacy, trade and war, with real-time battles and sieges between players. Made for a small group of friends, usually 2 or 3, in long sessions that are saved and resumed.

[b]V1 Beta.[/b] Every main feature is in, and three players have played it together over Steam. Expect bugs, and please send logs (at the bottom).

[h2]You need[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Songs of Syx V71, build 71.44[/b], on Windows, through Steam. Other builds aren't supported.
[*][b]SOSDET[/b], the deterministic engine this mod runs on. Subscribe to it too, and enable both in the launcher.
[*]The [b]same versions[/b] of both mods on every PC. Update both before you play.
[/list]

[h2]Playing[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Over Steam:[/b] the players are Steam friends. The host chooses Multiplayer, Create Lobby, then "create Steam lobby". The others choose "join through Steam", or accept the invite.
[*][b]By IP:[/b] the host's TCP port 47510 must be reachable, through a forwarded port or a VPN such as Radmin VPN.
[*]The host picks the map. Each player picks a race, a faction and a capital location, and NPC realms are generated around you.
[*]The host sets the speed, up to 250x, and the slowest PC caps it for everyone. Menus and popups never pause the game.
[/list]

[h2]Completed milestones[/h2]
[b]Playtest build (2026-10-29)[/b]
[list]
[*]Your own city with no delay, on one world map in lockstep on every PC
[*]Lobby and setup, Steam and direct IP, campaign saves and resume
[*]Diplomacy between players: treaties, money, goods, regions, vassals, passage
[*]Trade between players, with each player's own tariff
[*]War, and real-time battles where each player commands their own army
[*]Watching another player's city live, with their permission
[*]Going on without a player who drops out, and recovery from a desync
[/list]
[b]V1 Beta (2026-11-01)[/b]
[list]
[*]Sieges of player capitals: the wait, the sally, a real-time assault, then sack, vassalage or elimination
[*]Allies in battles and assaults, and a speed vote between commanders
[*]Battles against NPCs on their own clock while the world runs on, and watching them
[*]Chat, event notifications, and other players' cursors on maps and minimaps
[*]Eliminated players stay on as spectators
[*]Direct routes between players who start 6 to 15 tiles apart
[*]Global events, such as an eclipse, that reach every city at once
[*]25x on two PCs, and three players in one session over Steam

[h2]Known problems[/h2]
[list]
[*]At 250x, and at 25x in a big late-game city, the game runs only as fast as the slowest PC.
[*]The owner's game pauses for a few seconds when their city's view opens, and everyone else's for about a second.
[*]Goods or people crossing between a city and the world in the last half second before a campaign save can be lost on resume.
[*]Two players can end up with the same realm name.
[/list]
[b]Not planned:[/b] anti-cheat, joining a campaign in progress, AI for a disconnected player's city, compatibility with other mods.

[h2]Bug reports[/h2]
Every PC writes a log of each session. After a crash, a desync window or anything odd, close the game and send these folders, with a line about what happened and roughly when:
[list]
[*][b]%APPDATA%\songsofsyx\sosgsgmp-logs[/b]: the session logs
[*][b]%APPDATA%\songsofsyx\logs[/b]: the game's crash files
[/list]
