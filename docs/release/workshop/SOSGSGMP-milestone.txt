[h1]V1 Beta (2026-11-01)[/h1]

Every main feature of version 1 is in. Three players have played it together over Steam.

[h2]New since the playtest build[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Sieges of player capitals:[/b] the wait, the sally, a real-time assault with allied attackers, then sack, vassalage or elimination.
[*][b]Battles:[/b] allies between players and against NPCs, a speed vote between commanders, and a resync inside a battle.
[*][b]NPC battles on their own clock:[/b] the world runs on while you fight, and other players can watch.
[*][b]Chat and notifications:[/b] one chat for everyone, in the lobby and in the game, with wars, sieges and battles announced in it.
[*][b]Spectators:[/b] an eliminated player stays in the session and watches.
[*][b]Setup:[/b] Ready for every player, a confirmation on Leave, and a route notice while placing your capital.
[*][b]Direct routes:[/b] players who start 6 to 15 tiles apart get a direct road or sea crossing, so caravans no longer go the long way round. New worlds only.
[*][b]Global events:[/b] an eclipse, a comet, a great storm and other such events reach every city at the same moment.
[*][b]Cursors:[/b] other players' cursors now show on the minimaps too.
[*][b]Speed:[/b] 25x holds on two PCs.
[*][b]Safer installs:[/b] the game says so if SOSDET is off or the game's build is wrong, and the lobby refuses a player whose game, SOSDET or mod build differs.
[*][b]Fixed:[/b] the desync from NPC heirs' religions, found in the three-player playtest, and two desyncs after a city view or a local battle.
[/list]
