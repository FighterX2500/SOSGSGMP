# Batch 13, item 5: autosaves while paused, and broken autosaves (SOS-229)

Agent findings, 2026-11-18. Mod only, branch `b13-05`. No engine change. Updated with the owner's answers the same day.

## What the player saw

Their group paused for hours while away. The autosave kept going, every autosave slot held a broken save, and the campaign couldn't continue. Their logs and saves aren't here yet, so the causes below come from the code and the owner's logs of 17–18 Nov.

## Bug 1: autosaves counted while the world stood still

**Cause (proven from the code).** The host's autosave counted real time whenever the host's own game speed was above 0 (`MpSaves.autosave`, `GAME.SPEED.speed() == 0`). That speed doesn't say whether the world runs:

- **A pause that only one PC has.** `MpPause` doesn't send a pause press during any battle, a local NPC battle of any player included, nor for 2 s after one. Nor does any PC apply a pause it hears during a battle. The PC that pressed pause stops alone, and the chat says nothing. Every other PC, the host too, keeps its speed above 0, and the world waits for the paused PC.
- **The world held for other reasons.** A loading window, a lost connection, a disconnect window, or a slow PC holds every PC while the host's speed stays above 0.

So the host's count ran on through such a stand-still, however long, and an autosave came due.

**Fix.**
- A new plain class, `sosgsgmp.campaign.AutosaveClock`, counts real time only in frames where the world's step moved since the last frame, and only while the shared pause is off (`Pauses.pausedBy() == NOBODY`). The host's own speed no longer counts.
- The first step after a stand-still counts at most 1 s, so a long wait never counts.
- A waiting autosave (one waiting for a view, or one due before a pause) waits for the unpause too (`MpSaves.startWaiting`). A player's Save Campaign doesn't wait: it is ordered as before, and is taken once the game runs to its step.
- The panel line says "The campaign saves once the game is unpaused." while an autosave waits for that.

**Safeguard (owner, 2026-11-18).** An autosave that comes due while the world is still at the step of this PC's last campaign save is skipped, and the count starts again. The log says `an autosave skipped: the world is still at step N of the last campaign save`.

## Bug 2: why the saves were broken

**Not proven: it needs the player's logs and saves.** What the code shows:

- **No save can be written while every PC is stopped.** Every host-ordered save is for `stepPastPeers()`, a step no PC has reached yet. Every PC holds there and saves only once it has run to it. While paused, no PC steps, so the order waits. The one save at the current step is the save before a battle: it is made at the battle's hold, at most once per battle (`savedBeforeBattle`, `savedStep == ahead - 1`), and the battle needs a step to begin. So it can't repeat during a pause either.
- **So autosaves written during a "pause" mean the world ran on every PC.** The players likely saw a pause that the world didn't share. The likeliest way: a pause pressed or heard during a battle (above), or the speed set again by a load after a battle or view while the shared pause was on. With Bug 1 fixed, the autosave stops in every such case: it follows the shared pause and the step, not the speed. The pause itself is fixed too (below).
- **A save one PC couldn't make pushed good autosaves out (probable cause).** Each PC rotates its autosaves (`CampaignStore.rotate`) as soon as it has written its own save (`MpSaves.save`). It doesn't wait for every PC's report. Say one PC can't save, or saves a world that differs from the host's ("couldn't save: ...", "world differs"). Every other PC still drops its oldest autosave for one that can't be resumed with every player: that player's resume lobby says "You don't have save N". After as many failures as there are autosave slots, no autosave is left that every PC has. That fits "every autosave slot held a broken save".
- **Fix (owner, 2026-11-18: "keep newest good").** Each PC remembers the newest autosave that was done on every PC with no problem in this session (`MpSaves.goodAuto`, set when the save's outcome is empty). Rotation never removes it (`CampaignStore.rotate(id, keep, keepAlso)`): the newest `keep` autosaves stay as before, and that one stays past them. So there is at most one file more, and only while newer autosaves failed. The next good autosave takes its place. The state lives only in the session, not in any save, so there is no round trip to test: after a resume, the first good autosave sets it.
- **Other states a save could hold, checked:**
  - The game speed in a vanilla save is overwritten at resume (`EnginePlay.start`).
  - Orders given during a pause are stamped at steps past the pause. They apply before the save step or after it, as at any time.
  - Heartbeats run on the I/O thread, so a long pause doesn't drop a connection by itself.

## The shared pause during battles (owner, 2026-11-18: "fix in item 5")

**Cause (proven from the code).** `MpPause` treated every battle alike. During any battle (one every PC runs, or any player's local battle against NPCs) and for 2 s after, a press was not sent and a heard pause was not set. After the battle, nothing brought the PC back to the shared pause. So:
- A player not in the battle who pressed pause stopped alone, with no chat line. The world waited for that PC, and nobody else could unpause it.
- After a battle or a view's load set the speed again, a PC ran while the shared pause was on, or the other way round.

**Fix.** A new plain class, `sosgsgmp.session.PauseSync`, decides each frame what to send and what to set. `MpPause` feeds it and carries it out. The rules keep spec §4 and §8:
- **On the world, and in a battle every PC runs** (between players, a sally), a press is sent and a heard pause is set. In such a battle the shared pause pauses the battle on every PC, and any player unpauses it.
- **A battle against NPCs on this PC** (fought, watched or joined as an ally): the game's pause is that battle's own (spec §8). Nothing is sent or set; a heard pause waits.
- **While any player fights a battle against NPCs**, the world runs on at 1× (spec §4). A pause pressed on the world is sent and shown in the chat ("The game pauses once the battles against NPCs end."), but stops the game only when the last such battle ends. A press meanwhile turns that waiting pause on or off.
- **After a battle starts or ends, or a whole-game load** (`EnginePlay.loads()`), the speed settles for 2 s. Then the PC comes back to the shared pause. A pause the PC holds alone then counts as its player's press, since neither the engine nor a load ever pauses.
- A spectator's press is undone, as before.

**Spec:** §4 Pausing, one paragraph on battles.

## Tests

- `AutosaveClockTest` (7 tests): a running world counts real time. A world that stands still counts nothing for 15 minutes, whatever the host's speed: this is the player's case, and the old rule counted it. The shared pause counts nothing even if steps still run. The first step after a stand-still counts at most 1 s, and after a pause none. A restart counts from nothing. The safeguard: a world still at the last save's step hasn't moved.
- The old rule is in game-bound code (`MpSaves`, `GAME.SPEED`) and can't run in a unit test. The test shows the scenario the old rule counted.
- `CampaignStoreTest.failedAutosavesNeverRotateOutTheNewestOneEveryPcMade`: 3 slots, 3 good autosaves, then 6 failed ones: save 3 stays past the slots; the next good one takes its place.
- `PauseSyncTest` (8 tests): a press on the world is sent and a heard pause set; a spectator's press is undone. During another player's NPC battle a pause is sent and waits for its end: this is the old bug, where it wasn't sent. A press turns a waiting pause off. The fighting player's pause is their battle's own, and a heard pause waits until the battle is over and settled. In a battle every PC runs, the pause is shared, and the battle's load is no press. After a battle or a load the PC comes back to the shared pause. A pause pressed right after a battle counts once settled.
- The old `MpPause` rules are game-bound and can't run in a unit test. The tests show the scenarios they got wrong.
- Whole run: `gradlew.bat compileJava compileTestJava test build` (counts in the report).

## Test request

- **Setup:** 2 instances (twopc), host and one joiner, a fresh two-player game. In the host's game settings: Auto-Saving interval at its shortest (1 real minute) and 3 autosave files. Build from `b13-05` (or the batch C merge).
- **Steps:**
  1. Run 2 minutes unpaused: about 2 autosaves (`save N (AUTO) ordered`).
  2. **Joiner pauses** (autopilot `pause` on the joiner) for 3 minutes, then unpauses.
  3. **Host pauses** for 3 minutes, then unpauses.
  4. **A pause with an autosave waiting:** the host views the joiner's city (the joiner allows) for over a minute, so the host logs `a campaign save (AUTO) waits for the city views to end`. Pause, close the view, and stay paused 2 minutes. Then unpause.
  5. **A long pause:** pause for 15 real minutes, then unpause and run 30 s.
  6. **Save Campaign during a pause:** pause, the host presses Save Campaign (autopilot `save`), wait 30 s, then unpause. The save is taken once the game runs to its step.
  7. **Pause during a battle between players:** start one (the two players' armies meet). In the battle, the joiner pauses, then the host unpauses.
  8. **Pause during another player's NPC battle:** the joiner fights an NPC battle in real time. The host pauses on the world map. The joiner pauses and unpauses their battle. Then the joiner wins or retreats.
  9. **Right after a battle's load:** when step 7's battle ends and the world loads back, the joiner presses pause within 2 s. (A load during a pause can't happen: loads come at steps.)
  10. Quit both. Resume the save from step 6 with both PCs and run 3 minutes. Then resume the newest autosave and run 1 minute.
- **Pass:**
  - Between every `paused the game` line and the next `unpaused the game` line in the host's log, no `save N (AUTO) ordered` line.
  - In step 4, the host logs `a campaign save (AUTO) waits for the unpause` (or the view line), and the save is ordered only after the unpause.
  - Step 5 adds no autosave during the 15 minutes.
  - Every save is `done on every PC` with no problem.
  - Both resumes start, run, and show no desync (`desync` lines, the hash lines match on both PCs).
  - An `an autosave skipped` line is fine. It is not required.
  - Step 7: the chat says the joiner paused, the battle stops on both PCs, and the host's unpause runs it on both, with no desync.
  - Step 8: the chat says the host paused, with "The game pauses once the battles against NPCs end". The world and the joiner's battle run on. The joiner's battle pause pauses only their battle. When the battle ends, both PCs pause within about 2 s.
  - Step 9: about 2 s after the load, the joiner's pause is sent (the chat says the joiner paused) and both PCs pause. No load adds an "unpaused the game" line anywhere in the run.
  - Outside step 8's battle, no PC runs for more than about 2 s while another is paused (the step numbers in both logs stop together).
- **Keep:** both PCs' `sosgsgmp\logs` of the session and the resumes, and the host's `sosgsgmp\campaigns` records.
- **New tooling:** none.
