# Batch 13, item 3: restart a battle by vote (SOS-231)

## What the owner asked

Every battle can be restarted: a battle between players, a battle against NPCs, and the assault on a capital. Every
player in the session votes, those outside the battle and spectators too. More than half must say Yes. A tie fails. No
answer in 30 seconds counts as No. Spec section 8's "No restart" (SOS-93) is replaced.

## How it works

The vote is the mod's. The restart is the engine's.

1. A player who sees a battle calls the vote. They press the battle's own Restart (vanilla's button, which SOSDET now
   turns into a call), or Restart battle on the multiplayer panel. The panel's button also covers the assault's
   defender, whose game has no Restart of its own.
2. The call goes to the host. The host opens the vote for every player in the session and tells every PC. A second call
   while one is open is refused, to its caller only.
3. Every player but the caller gets a window: Yes or No, with the seconds left. The caller votes Yes by calling.
4. The host counts. The vote passes as soon as more than half say Yes. It fails as soon as that can't happen, or when
   30 seconds are up. A player who leaves the session no longer counts.
5. Every frame while a vote is open, the host asks its engine whether the battle can still restart. If the battle ended,
   or a player joins or leaves it, the vote is called off.
6. When the vote passes, the host writes a restart into the engine's AI log. Every PC applies it at the same step.

What each PC does at that step:

- **Battle between players, or a player's own battle** (runs on every PC): every PC reloads the battle's start save,
  which every PC already holds. Both sides deploy again.
- **Battle against NPCs** (runs on the fighter's PC; allies and watchers follow it): the fighter saves its world, loads
  the battle as generated, and puts the world back in. Its watchers load the fighter's new save, the way a viewer does
  when another player joins (SOS-151). Every other PC reloads its own game, so all hashes stay the same.
- **Assault**: the defender saved its city right after the invasion started. It loads that save. Its viewers load the
  same save. Every other PC reloads its own game. The world stood still during the assault, so nothing else goes back.

The engine refuses a restart that would break the lockstep: the battle is over or still being set up, a player is
joining or leaving it, or a battle prompt is open on the PC that would load. The same checks run on every PC at the
step, so every PC agrees. A refused restart becomes a chat line on every PC.

## Test request for the lead

- setup: `battles3`, three PCs, SOSDET and the mod from b13-03 on every PC.
- steps:
  1. A battle between two players. Press Restart in the battle. Vote Yes on the other two PCs.
  2. Again, and vote No on both. Then again with a tie (if two players: one Yes, one No).
  3. An NPC battle, with a second player watching it. Call the vote from the watcher's PC. Vote Yes.
  4. An assault on a player's capital. Call it from the defender's panel (Restart battle). Vote Yes.
  5. Once, let the window run out without answering.
- pass: Yes restarts the battle on every PC that runs it, back to deployment (or the assault's start). The chat says it
  restarted. No desync, and the same hash on every PC after the restart. No, a tie, and no answer restart nothing, and
  the chat says the vote failed.
- keep: every PC's playtest log (lines with `restart vote` and `restart of`), and the engine's logs.
- new tooling: `battles3` needs a way to call and answer a vote by autopilot, if it should run without hands.

## Open points

- "Any player can call the vote" is read as: any player who sees the battle (in it, an ally, a watcher, a viewer of it,
  or a spectator watching it). A player elsewhere has no battle to name.
- A player who is gone but not yet removed (the host still waits) counts as No when the time runs out.
- Orders already stamped after the restart step apply to the restarted battle. They are a few steps at most.
- An ally who left an NPC battle and closed their view leaves their divisions without a commander after a restart.
- Loading a world into a battle game on the fighter's PC is checked by reasoning only.
