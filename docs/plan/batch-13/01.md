# Batch 13, item 1: a losing player gets spoils from an NPC battle (SOS-225)

## What was seen

After a battle against NPCs that the player lost, a caravan with spoils went to the losing player's city. No window showed for it.

The host's log of 2026-11-17 has such caravans. At 23:10:36 two "Battle: Spoils" caravans left for FighterX2500, 39 steps apart (steps 134772 and 134811). They held one army's whole kit (260 Plate Armour, 260 Shield, 258 Spear, food). No result window logs a battle there, so the log alone doesn't show which battle it was.

## Cause (proven from the source; not yet seen in a game)

The guess in the plan was wrong. The battle's spoils never go to `FACTIONS.player()` after a defeat. Both vanilla and the engine send them to the winner's side (`ResolverPlayer.Res.shipRetreat` → `Util.ship(ee.side, …)`). The caravan comes from the beaten army's own supplies:

1. A real-time battle's result (`ResolverPlayer.Manual`, both an Engage and an NPC battle on its own clock) resolves only the men (`WDIV.resolve`). Every army keeps all its dead men's gear. The winner's spoils are counted from that same gear (`ResolverUnit.count`, 40%), so the gear is counted twice.
2. An army with no men left outside its own land is removed (`WArmy.update`). Removing it disbands its divisions, so its supply targets drop to 0. Then `checkForResources` → `returnResources` ships everything it holds to its human's capital as a spoils caravan. No window shows; vanilla has none for this either.
3. An auto-resolve doesn't do this. Its `extract` takes the dead's gear (1.1 times the share dead) before anything else, so a destroyed army holds nothing.

battles3 already noticed it. Its `captives()` check said "a destroyed army's own supplies go home as vanilla's spoils shipments too", so it checked only captives for losers.

The engine's assault has the same gap (`GameAssault`). When the defender holds, the besieging armies are disbanded while they still hold their gear, which had also gone into the invasion's loot. So the beaten attacker got a caravan home. When the assault is retreated or won, the dead's gear stays.

## The fix (SOSDET, branch b13-01)

- `BattleRules.deadGear(current, lost, men)` is plain arithmetic, with unit tests: what vanilla's auto-resolve takes, 1.1 times the share dead, capped at the whole. An army with no men keeps nothing.
- `Hooks.deadGear(army, lost)` takes that part of each supply from a world army. It works only in a session, inside the step, alike on every PC.
- Patch `15-humans`, `ResolverPlayer.Manual`: `afterBattle` keeps each army's losses (`sosdetLostE`, `sosdetLostP`). The result's accept calls `deadGear` for every army on both sides after the shipping and before the divisions resolve, in the same order as the auto-resolve's `extract`. With several winners it runs once, in the last accept.
- `GameAssault`: retreated or taken, each army loses its dead's gear. Held, the lost armies lose all of it before the disband.
- `docs/hooks.md` in SOSDET has a row for the new hook use.

So survivors keep their gear and the dead lose it. A destroyed army ships nothing home. The winner's spoils are unchanged.

The change runs inside the step on every PC, from the same world state, so every PC takes the same gear. The state hash doesn't read army supplies directly. It catches a difference only later, through what the supplies change: caravans, city goods, an army's power. Single player is unchanged: `deadGear` does nothing without a session (owner, 2026-11-18: multiplayer only).
