# Batch 13, item 10: helping another player, and troops in another player's city

Cards: SOS-232 (10a), SOS-233 (10c), SOS-234 (10b). Branches `b13-10` in the mod and in SOSDET.

## 10a: allies in the Attack against an invasion

### Found

- The raids of the 2026-11-17 playtest were invasions of a player's capital ("capital is invaded by Raiders" in the host logs, 21:55, 00:20, 01:44). The player fights them with vanilla's **Attack** in the invasion message: the sally outside the walls (SOS-145).
- In a session the Attack is the world command 7968 (`MapBattles.INVASION_SALLY`) and runs as a local battle on the defender's PC. Its battle is vanilla's `Attack` constructor: the capital's garrison and the defender's own armies within 4 tiles of the capital (`Attack.armies`, faction == player). Nobody else joins. SOS-145 chose this on purpose ("no ally prompt", batch-7.md).
- Vanilla's after-battle (`Attack.afterExit`, in the defender's city) gives the whole spoils window (`MVictory2`: loot, the invaders' gear by the share that died, and captives by race) to the defender.
- **The raider's stash** is the raider's personal treasury (`Raider.bounty`). Vanilla pays it in `MessDefeated`, when the raid's state reaches DEFEATED after the invasion listeners hear the victory. In a session that runs in the world part as the defender (raids are per human), so it already goes to the defender alone. Nothing changes there.

### The fix (SOSDET)

- **The question.** The sally command now carries the invader's faction (-1 for raiders and rebels), read on the defender's PC from its invasion. When it applies (world part, every PC, as the defender), the engine floods 4 tiles along the map from the capital, as `Attack.armies` does, and finds every other human's army there (`GameSally.near`). Each human found is asked when they are allied with the defender, at war with the invader (raiders: always), not in a local battle, not removed or frozen (`SallyLayout.asked`). If nobody is, the sally engages at once, as before.
- Otherwise the question is the open world prompt (`SallyLayout.Ask`): the asked players get the existing Join / Stay out window (`MpAllyJoin`, SOS-118), the defender's armies and theirs wait in place, and it engages at the next step once everyone answered, or after 30 s (the prompt timer). A second Attack meanwhile is skipped. While another world prompt is open, nobody is asked and the sally engages at once (one prompt at a time).
- **The battle.** At Engage (every PC), `SallyLayout` holds who brings what: each commander's armies near the capital, their divisions within the side's slots (as in a battle with allies, but the defender's whole garrison always fits, since vanilla's after-battle reads every garrison division), and each one's power. The division owners go into the roster (`BattleAllies.owners`), so each player commands their own divisions and fights with their own bonuses, and every ally's PC follows the battle as in SOS-160. The battle PC builds the allies' divisions in the world part at Engage, on its own stream, as a world battle's Engage builds its sides; every PC reseeds the world stream after it alike.
- **Patch `99-world-battles`, `Attack.java`** (extended, three hook lines): the constructor caps the defender's own army divisions at its slots (`sallyOwnDivisions`) and adds the allies' divisions after them (`sallyAllies`); the after-battle keeps the defender's share before its window (`sallySpoils`). Without allies the hooks change nothing.
- **The result.** In the world part on every PC, each ally's divisions get their survivors (a count and mean experience) directly, and only the defender's go to its city (`SALLY_RESULT`). In the defender's city vanilla computes the spoils; `sallySpoils` splits every tradable by power (`BattleRules.share`), keeps the defender's share for vanilla's window, and puts each ally's share in the city's report (new input `SALLY_SPOILS`). When the report applies, every PC ships it by caravan from the defender's capital to the ally's, as a battle's spoils travel.
- **Power** is each player's strength at Engage as the game measures it: `AD.power` of the armies they brought, plus the garrison's region power (`RD.MILITARY().power`) for the defender. Both are world values the NPC AI already reads, so every PC has the same. The remainder goes to the most power, the defender on a tie; a player whose army left the field gets nothing.

### The owner's answers (2026-11-18)

1. The three hook lines in SOSDET patch `99-world-battles` (`Attack.java`): "Approve". SOSDET `docs/hooks.md` says so.
2. Scope: "Every invasion": raiders and NPC invaders of a capital, as built. An ally is asked when at war with the invader; raiders count as at war with everyone.
3. and 4. The v1 shortcuts: "Accept both". While another world prompt is open, the Attack starts at once and nobody is asked. An ally's share goes by caravan, with no window.
5. Power and remainder: "Confirm". Power is the armies' `AD.power`, plus the garrison's region power for the defender; the remainder goes to the most power, the defender on a tie; a player whose army left gets nothing.

### The city report's format

The new input kind `SALLY_SPOILS` is appended to `InputKind`, so every older kind keeps its number and a report written before it reads back the same. The report's `PROTOCOL_VERSION` (7) stays: a world save keeps the reports of frozen players (SOSDET `GameFrozen`, format 2), and raising the version would refuse those saves for no reason. A report holding the new kind is only ever made by this build, and every PC in a session runs the same build (the game-files check, SOS-159), so no older reader meets one. An older engine would refuse it by its range check, not misread it.

### Gaps

- Not run in a game: everything above rests on the unit tests and reading. ## 10c: an ally's army stationed at another player's capital

[ADR 0011](../../decisions/0011-allied-army-stationed-at-a-capital.md), accepted by the owner on 2026-11-18 with nine answers (recorded there): option A on the map; allies, colleagues, overlords and vassals; no prompt; the army stays where it stands at war; a frozen host ends it; the host may supply it; someone else's war stays out; a limit by free slots; the 10b view is automatic. Spec §9 "Allied armies at a capital", §11 (freezing and removal) and §15 (an [AT] line) say it.

### The fix (SOSDET)

- `StationBook` (plain Java): the records (army index and iteration, its human, the host, its tile, the step), the rules (`refusal`: own army, not yet stationed, another human's capital, nobody frozen or removed, the agreement and no war, within 3 tiles, standing still, soldiers in it, free slots; `endReason`: army gone, a human away, the capital lost, the agreement ended or a war, moved), save as ints with a version, and a hash.
- `GameStations`: the save section `SOSDET_STATIONS` (in the world save and `GameWorldLoad.KEYS`, after `SOSDET_FROZEN`; a save without it has none), the world-hash part (a game with none hashes as before), the rules read from the world, and `endDue`, run in the world part after the commands at every step outside a battle, on every PC. The host's own divisions are its capital's garrison and its armies'; the slots are `DIVISIONS_PER_ARMY` (120).
- World commands 7980 station, 7981 end (the host sends it away, its player calls it back), 7982 supply (`Stations`).
- The host's Supply: 7982 queues `TxnKind.STATION_SUPPLY` to the host's city; its city tail fills each supply from the stockpiles, as much as they hold, and the supply counter sends it to the world as a `SUPPLY` input in the next report (the path `ARMY_SUPPLY_DIV` already takes). Exactly once: the transaction is applied once by its id, the report's inputs once in report order, and the city's supply ledger counts what is on its way.
- Notices 13 `STATIONED`, 14 `STATION_ENDED` (with the reason) and 15 `STATION_SUPPLIED`, for the chat.
- `Engine`: `stationRefusal`, `station`, `stationEndRefusal`, `stationEnd`, `stationSupplyRefusal`, `stationSupply`, `stationedCount`, `stationedArmy`, `stationedOwner`, `stationedHost`. Docs: `api.md`, `commands.md`, `hooks.md` (the section list).
- No game-file patch: everything is world commands, a save section and engine code.

## 10b: fighting inside the city

Not built yet. The plan, from reading the source; each point says whether it needs a change to a game file.

1. **When the stationed divisions come in.** At an NPC invasion of the host's capital, the world part already sends `INVADE` to the host's city (`Hooks.invade`, patch 14). With it, the world part builds the stationed armies' divisions (`WDIV.generate`, as the assault and 10a do) and sends them in a new transaction (`STATION_DIVS`). The host's city keeps them until the invaders deploy, then puts them in free slots of army 0 as `HTYPES.SOLDIER` people (the battle map's player-side kind, battle AI only), placed and mustered as `DivDeployer.place` does for invaders. Their deploy moment is `Invasion.launch` (`settlement/battle/invasion/Invasion.java:234`, private): read by reflection of the invasion's `state` in the city part, no hook. For a player's assault, the stationed divisions go in at the assault's start in the city tail (`Session.assaultBegin`), with the defender's.
2. **Who commands them.** The commanders table already gates the city's battle UI by division (`OwnArmy.selectable`, used in the assault, SOS-92): the stationed divisions are their player's there. In an NPC invasion, the stationed player views the host's city with no Allow (answer 9: the mod's `MpViews` allows it at once), and their orders go to the host's PC, which applies them at a step as its own (SOSDET plan 0019's route for an ally's orders). Engine and mod code only.
3. **Keeping them theirs in the host's city.** Vanilla's battle AI drops a soldier from a division over its target (`AIModule_Battle.java:88`), so the target is set to the men put in. Vanilla's recruiting then refills a division under its target from the city's citizens (`settlement/battle/ArmyTrainingInfo.java:322`, `RaceDiv.getNext`). Keeping the target equal to the men left after each death, from engine code in the city part, may race with that recruiting in the same update. **This is the one place a hook may be needed:** `ArmyTrainingInfo.RaceDiv.getNext` skipping a stationed division (`if (sosdet.Hooks.stationedDiv(div)) { divs.removeLast(); continue; }`), in a game file neither SOSDET nor the mod patches today. No API or reflection route can stop the loop from picking that division. The host's own edits to those divisions (size, training, send out) are commands, which the engine's appliers refuse for a stationed division: no hook.
4. **The end.** At the invasion's end, the city's existing `Invador.tell` hook (patch 99) already reports to the world part; with it, the host's city removes the stationed people and reports each stationed division's survivors (`DIV_RESOLVE_COUNT`, as a local battle does), and the world part resolves them into the stationed army's divisions. For an assault, the assault's own end does the same.
5. **Not known without a game:** whether `SOLDIER` people in a city (not the battle map) stay out of the population, needs, housing and work counts while they are there (ADR 0011's spike 1). It decides whether point 1 holds or the stationed people need a kind of their own, which would mean hooks in `HTYPES` and `AIModules`.
