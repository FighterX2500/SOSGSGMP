# Batch 13, item 10: helping another player, and troops in another player's city

Cards: SOS-232 (10a), SOS-233 (10c), SOS-234 (10b). Branches `b13-10` in the mod and in SOSDET.

## 10a: allies in the Attack against an invasion

### Found

- The raids of the 2026-11-17 playtest were invasions of a player's capital ("capital is invaded by Raiders" in the host logs, 21:55, 00:20, 01:44). The player fights them with vanilla's **Attack** in the invasion message: the sally outside the walls (SOS-145).
- In a session the Attack is the world command 7968 (`MapBattles.INVASION_SALLY`) and runs as a local battle on the defender's PC. Its battle is vanilla's `Attack` constructor: the capital's garrison and the defender's own armies within 4 tiles of the capital (`Attack.armies`, faction == player). Nobody else joins. SOS-145 chose this on purpose ("no ally prompt", batch-7.md).
- Vanilla's after-battle (`Attack.afterExit`, in the defender's city) gives the whole spoils window (`MVictory2`: loot, the invaders' gear by the share that died, and captives by race) to the defender.
- **The raider's stash** is the raider's personal treasury (`Raider.bounty`). Vanilla pays it in `MessDefeated`, when the raid's state reaches DEFEATED after the invasion listeners hear the victory. In a session that runs in the world part as the defender (raids are per human), so it already goes to the defender alone. Nothing changes there.

### The fix (SOSDET)

- **The question.** The sally command now carries the invader's faction (-1 for raiders and rebels), read on the defender's PC from its invasion. When it applies (world part, every PC, as the defender), the engine floods 4 tiles along the map from the capital, as `Attack.armies` does, and finds every other human's army there (`GameSally.near`). Each human found is asked when they are allied with the defender, at war with the invader (raiders: always), not in a local battle, not removed or frozen (`SallyLayout.asked`). If nobody is, the sally engages at once, as before.
- Otherwise the question is the open world prompt (`SallyLayout.Ask`): the asked players get the existing Join / Stay out window (`MpAllyJoin`, SOS-118), the defender's armies and theirs wait in place, and it engages at the next step once everyone answered, or after 30 s (the prompt timer). A second Attack meanwhile is skipped. While another world prompt is open, nobody is asked and the sally engages at once (one prompt at a time).
- **The battle.** At Engage (every PC), `SallyLayout` holds who brings what: each commander's armies near the capital, their divisions within the side's slots (as in a battle with allies, but the defender's whole garrison always fits, since vanilla's after-battle reads every garrison division), and each one's power. The division owners go into the roster (`BattleAllies.owners`), so each player commands their own divisions and fights with their own bonuses, and every ally's PC follows the battle as in SOS-160. The battle PC builds the allies' divisions in the world part at Engage, on its own stream, as a world battle's Engage builds its sides; every PC reseeds the world stream after it alike.
- **Patch `99-world-battles`, `Attack.java`** (extended, three hook lines): the constructor caps the defender's own army divisions at its slots (`sallyOwnDivisions`) and adds the allies' divisions after them (`sallyAllies`); the after-battle keeps the defender's share before its window (`sallySpoils`). Without allies the hooks change nothing.
- **The result.** In the world part on every PC, each ally's divisions get their survivors (a count and mean experience) directly, and only the defender's go to its city (`SALLY_RESULT`). In the defender's city vanilla computes the spoils; `sallySpoils` splits every tradable by power (`BattleRules.share`), keeps the defender's share for vanilla's window, and puts each ally's share in the city's report (new input `SALLY_SPOILS`). When the report applies, every PC ships it by caravan from the defender's capital to the ally's, as a battle's spoils travel.
- **Power** is each player's strength at Engage as the game measures it: `AD.power` of the armies they brought, plus the garrison's region power (`RD.MILITARY().power`) for the defender. Both are world values the NPC AI already reads, so every PC has the same. The remainder goes to the most power, the defender on a tie; a player whose army left the field gets nothing.

### Tests

- Engine unit tests, `SallyLayoutTest` (8): the 4-tile rule (4.0 asked, 4.4 not), who is eligible and each once, slot splits, the garrison always fitting, owners by position, the split by power with its remainder and tie, an ally who left getting nothing with nothing lost, and the share's report input round trip (bytes, then a whole `CityReport`).
- Engine `gradlew.bat build`: 387 tests, 0 failures. Mod `gradlew.bat build` against the engine worktree: 312 tests, one loopback test timed out under load (`LoopbackSetupTest.aPlayerWhoLeavesFrozen...`), 52 of 52 in that class on its own run.
- Test tooling: the mod's autopilot `sallyseen` prints the last sally's layout and every share shipped; `allies` now also prints the sally's question (armies found, their distances, who was asked).

### Test request

- **Setup:** `raid3.py` (three games, campaign save 77, local battles on), build from `b13-10` of both repositories. Before the raid, in each game's autopilot: game 2 and game 3 `ally` with the host (the host `accept`s), or use a save where they are allied. Find the host's capital centre (`regionat <index>` of the host's capital, or the host's `status`). Game 2 `teleportnear` a tile about 2 to 3 tiles from it; game 3 `teleportnear` a tile about 8 tiles from it.
- **Steps:** the raiders invade the host's capital; the host `invasionattack`. Game 2 answers `join`. Every game: `allies`. Let the battle run (the host and game 2 `charge`; the host `rout` once nobody else watches, as raid3 does) and close it; the host `victoryaccept`. 30 s after the battle's end, every game: `sallyseen`.
- **Pass:**
  - `allies` on every game shows the same line: game 2's army near and asked, game 3's not asked (its army isn't within 4, or isn't found).
  - Game 2 gets the Join window, game 3 doesn't; game 2's PC follows the battle (a view), and game 2's divisions take its orders.
  - `sallyseen` shows the same layout and the same shares on every game, game 2's share is shipped once, and the host's line says it keeps the rest. The shares follow the two powers in the line.
  - No desync for 5 minutes after; a caravan of spoils goes to game 2's capital.
- **Keep:** the three playtest logs, the autopilot events, a screenshot of game 2's Join window and of the host's victory window.
- **New tooling:** the autopilot command `sallyseen` (no new script).

### Gaps

- Not run in a game: everything above rests on the unit tests and reading. The battle's division positions are fixed at Engage from the garrison in the defender's report; if the garrison changes between Engage and the freeze step, the allies' divisions shift by that many places (owners and survivors off by a few). Rare (a few seconds), and the same on every PC.
- If an ally or the defender is removed, or the world reloads, while the allies are being asked, the question closes and the sally doesn't start; the defender presses Attack again.
- The defender sees no "waiting for allies" while they answer (up to 30 s). The chat says who joined and who stayed out when it engages.

## 10c: an ally's army stationed at another player's capital

The ADR for the owner's approval: [ADR 0011](../../decisions/0011-allied-army-stationed-at-a-capital.md). It proposes option A: the ally's army stays a world army stationed at the capital, a world record in lockstep; nothing of it runs inside the host's city until a fight there (10b). Its open questions go to the owner through the lead.

A source check that needed no game: `Config.battle().DIVISIONS_PER_ARMY` is 120 in V71's `data/assets/init/config/Battle.txt` (200 men each), so army 0 has 120 division slots that 10b's stationed divisions would share with the host's own.

No spike code: the ADR's remaining no-game questions are source reading, listed there; the rest need a game.
