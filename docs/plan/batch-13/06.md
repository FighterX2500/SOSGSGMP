# Batch 13, item 6: the Goods tab's Price column (SOS-230)

Date: 2026-11-18. Branch `b13-06`. Mod only.

## What was there

Another player's Goods tab had one Price column: their lowest export price plus the toll. Two things were off:

- It showed only one direction. Nothing said what this player gets selling to them.
- Its toll was vanilla's (`TradeManager.toll`, rounded), not the market's between players (`DepotPrice.toll`: rounded down, with the buyer's reported roads). So the tab and the Import depot could differ by 1 for the same goods.

## The rule (plan, item 6)

The column is named Price, with two subcolumns:

- **Buy:** what this player would pay the other for one unit.
- **Sell:** what this player would get selling to them.

The plan's words for Buy are "their export price + toll + this player's tariff". That doesn't match spec §7: the buyer's tariff is not added to what the buyer pays. It is cut from what reaches the seller and paid back to the buyer's treasury (owner, 2026-11-04; SOS-138; SOSDET `TariffBook.cut`). No sale charges export price + toll + tariff, and no depot shows that figure. So the code shows Buy as export price + toll, the price the Import depot lists the player at, and the question went to the lead (below).

## The fix

- `MpPlayerWindow`: the Goods tab's header is Resource, Stored, Price (Buy, Sell under it), Exported, Imported. Each Buy and Sell figure is its own hoverable cell (`PriceCell`).
  - Buy: `MpPlayers.depotPrice(f, t, true)`, the Import depot's price for that player. Blank while their Export depot doesn't take the goods, or before their first report.
  - Sell: `MpPlayers.depotReceived(f, t)`, new: this player's export price less their tariff on this player, the Export depot hover's "Reaches you". Blank while their Import depot doesn't take the goods.
  - A figure is dark (the game's inactive text colour) while `MpPlayers.depotOverCap` says the price, the toll included, is at or over the buyer's highest import price: the same test that darkens the depot's row.
  - A figure's hover is a line saying what it is, then the depot row's own hover (`MpPlayers.depotHover`): export price, toll, what is paid, the tariff, what it costs or what reaches you, the caps, and the other depot's goods or room. When the figure is blank, the hover says why.
  - The rest of the row keeps the traded hover (this year, last year, all time). The old hover's buy and sell prices and "Their tariff on you" are now in the figures' hovers.
- `DepotPrice.received(asked, percent)`: the arithmetic for Sell, without a value, as `paid(asked, toll)` is for Buy. The instance method uses it. Plain Java, tested.
- One authority: the tab and the depots call the same `MpPlayers` methods, so their figures can't drift.
- Autopilot GUI preview: two new steps after the depot hovers. Step 19 opens the other player's Goods tab with the Buy hover, step 20 with the Sell hover, for the goods the depot hovers of steps 17 and 18 used. Each writes a `goodsprice` event with the goods, the figure and whether it is dark. `twopc.py --gui-preview` help says so.
- Spec §7: a new bullet, "Prices between players".
