# Batch 13, item 9: betrayal after an NPC broke the treaty (SOS-227)

Date: 2026-11-18. Branches: mod `b13-09` (this file and the spec), engine `b13-09` (the fix).

## Found

- Vanilla's `OpsStance.setNewStance(npc, stance, playerDidIt)` never reads `playerDidIt`.
- A war declaration's betrayal comes from `betrayalD`: from neutral it is -1, scaled by the treaty's freshness. Freshness is the time since the last stance change between the two, `DIP.secondSinceStance`, whoever made it.
- An NPC's court cancels a treaty in `Stance.messDown` with `downTo.set(fa)`, straight into `DIP`, not through `setNewStance`. That change still resets the stance's time.
- So a war on an NPC that has just cancelled its trade treaty counts as a fresh betrayal, the full -1, though the player broke nothing.

## Cause

Proven from the source (V71.44): `DIP.set` stamps `secondOfStance` for every change, and `betrayalD` reads only that stamp. Nothing records who made the change. Not reproduced in a game; the rig run below is the reproduction and the check.

## The rule (owner, 2026-11-18)

- Breaking a treaty yourself, then declaring war, still costs betrayal.
- A war on an NPC that broke its treaties with you costs none.
- Like SOS-141, it changes vanilla in every game with the mods, single player too.

How the fix reads "broke its treaties": the NPC's own lowering of the stance was the last change, and it left no treaty, so the two are neutral. A war that breaks a treaty still standing (the NPC went from allies to colleagues only) is the player's own break and costs vanilla's betrayal. The owner confirmed that reading (below).

## The fix (engine)

- `BreakBook` (plain Java): per human and NPC, whether the last stance change was one the human didn't make that lowered the stance. War is the lowest stance; the others go by vanilla's loyalty. Any later change clears the mark.
- `GameBreaks`: the book as a world save section, `SOSDET_BREAKS`, book version 1. It hears every stance change through the game's own `DIP.DipActivityListener`, so the NPC's cancels need no new hook. A faction leaving or a new one in its slot drops its pairs.
- Patch `14-report-reads`, its `OpsStance` part, two lines:
  - `setNewStance` marks its change as the human's after the stance is set. Every change a human makes with an NPC goes through it: deals, the war from a siege order, vassalage.
  - `betrayalD` gives 0 for a war on an NPC marked, when the two are neutral. The hover and the AI's deal values read the same function, so they agree.
- Per human: the pair is the human's slot and the NPC's slot. Another human's breaks don't count.
- World state: saved with the world (`GameWorldLoad.KEYS`), so a resync and a view carry it; in the world hash with the stances (part 3), only when not empty.
- A save without the section (any one before this) has no marks: the first war after the load costs vanilla's betrayal, until an NPC breaks a stance again.
- No mod code changes. The engine's `docs/hooks.md` has the new row.

## The owner's answers (2026-11-18)

- When a war is free: **no treaty left**, as built. The NPC's own lowering of the stance was the last change between the two, and they are neutral. A war that breaks a treaty still standing (the NPC went from allies to colleagues only) costs betrayal as in vanilla.
- A stance passed to a player's NPC vassal (SOS-140) that goes back when its source ends counts as the NPC's break, so a war right after costs nothing: **accepted**.
- The spec's §6 bullet states both. No code changes followed.
