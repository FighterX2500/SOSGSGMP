# Batch 13, item 2: vassals and players' friends (SOS-226)

Owner, 2026-11-18: Colleagues isn't passed from a player's own NPC vassals to that player's player friends. Vassals still ask money to keep relations and break treaties with them. New rules: a passed stance is locked while it's passed, and an alliance between players passes Colleagues to each one's vassals.

Branches `b13-02` in the mod and in SOSDET.

## What happened

Fray2's logs of 2026-11-17, sent to the host (`sosgsgmp\reports\6a3dfa272df0eaf1\1-Fray2\`). FighterX2500 has two NPC vassals, Ethimelle and Bromia; FighterX2500 and Fray2 are colleagues.

- 18:12:32 "Ethimelle is now your colleague, through your agreement with FighterX2500." Passing works.
- 18:23:55 FighterX2500 and Fray2 become allies. At 18:24:28 they go back to Colleagues, and Ethimelle is passed again at 18:24:33. No "no longer" notice came in between, so before 18:23 the pair was no longer the book's: someone had changed the stance (see cause 2).
- 22:17:02 (log `20261117-214453`) and 22:21:51 (log `20261117-222020`): FighterX2500 and Fray2 become allies, and at once "Bromia is no longer your trade partner or colleague: the agreement with its overlord ended."

## Causes (proven from the logs and the code)

1. **The alliance took the stance away.** `StanceBook.passes` passed a trade agreement and Colleagues only. When two colleagues became allies, what passed became nothing, and the passed Colleagues went back to what Fray2 and Bromia had before. That is the old rule (spec §6: "An alliance never passes"), and it is what the owner saw as Colleagues not passing.
2. **The vassal's court asked money, then cancelled.** Vanilla's diplomacy event (`EventDiplomacy` → `Stance.process`) runs for each human. When an NPC's opinion of a player is under 75% of what the stance needs, `Stance.messDown` first sends "Relations Worsen", a deal asking money to keep the stance, and the next time cancels it one step down. A vassal's opinion of its overlord's friend is low, so this happened to every passed stance. The book then saw a stance changed by someone else and let it go for good (the old rule: "theirs from then on"). That explains Ethimelle before 18:23.
3. **Why SOS-146 didn't stop it.** It isn't a regression of SOS-146's code: SOS-146 kept a friend's vassal out of `DipWarPlayer`'s potential enemies, which covers the war timer's Demand, its wars and its harm. `Stance.messDown` is another vanilla path, which SOS-146 never covered.

## The fix

Engine (SOSDET):

- `StanceBook`: a new code `ALLY`; `ABOVE` now means a vassal's or an overlord's stance only. `passes(ALLY)` is Colleagues. A vassalage between two players still passes nothing.
- `StanceBook.decide`, the lock: a passed stance that is lowered (by the vassal's court, or by the player's own diplomacy) is set back to the passed one at the next step. A war, or a higher stance the player and the vassal agree on, is theirs: the book lets go of the pair with nothing to set back. After a peace the stance passes again, as "a war blocks it until they make peace" says. The released flag is gone.
- The book's save format goes to version 2 (no released flag). A version 1 book loads; a pair it had released is left out, and passes afresh at the next step if it still should, with the stance it has then as its stance before.
- `HumanDeals.code` maps an alliance to `ALLY`.
- Patch `98-world-fixes`, new file `game/events/faction/player/Stance.java`: `messDown` does nothing for an NPC whose overlord is another human with a trade, colleague or alliance stance with the human current (`Hooks.friendOfOverlord`, SOS-146's). No money asked, nothing cancelled. Its welcome and its proposals to raise a stance stay vanilla. This is a new hook site, approved by the owner (2026-11-18). `docs/hooks.md` has its row.

Mod:

- Spec §6: an alliance passes Colleagues; a passed stance is locked while it's passed; a friend's vassal asks no money to keep a stance and cancels none.
- Test tooling: `diplo` also shows each player's stance with the other players' NPC vassals (`41=npc-Colleagues`).

## The owner's answers (2026-11-18)

1. The `Stance.messDown` hook in `98-world-fixes`: approved.
2. The lock holds against everyone: a passed stance the player lowers on their own is set back too, as the vassal's court's would be.
3. The no-demand rule covers every stance between a vassal and its overlord's friends, not only passed ones.
4. Confirmed as built: after a war and a peace the stance passes again; a higher stance the player and the vassal agree on is theirs and stays when the agreement ends; and the spec §6 wording for both.

## Tests

Unit (`StanceBookTest`, 16 tests): an alliance passes Colleagues; colleagues who become allies keep the passed Colleagues (the playtest's bug); a lowered passed stance is set back, by the court or by the player; a higher stance of their own is theirs and stays when the source ends; a war lets go and the stance passes again after the peace; the version 2 round trip; a version 1 book loads without its released pairs; bad books are refused.

Not unit-tested: the `Stance.messDown` hook and `HumanDeals.code`, which need a running game. The test request covers them.
